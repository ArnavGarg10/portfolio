Built a dual-system framework for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 to predict shark foraging behavior using real satellite data. Developed a predictive mathematical model using NASA SWOT and PACE OCI mission data with Haversine distance calculations to locate warm ocean eddies and chlorophyll hotspots. Designed a custom hardware tracking tag using a Raspberry Pi, accelerometer, and camera with YOLO object detection for real-time prey identification at the edge. Built a Flask web interface supporting coordinate input, dynamic map generation with Matplotlib and Cartopy, and batch CSV upload processing. Won 1st Place out of 69 teams at the NASA Space Apps Mountain View event, competing against university teams from MIT and UC Berkeley as a high school freshman, and was selected as a Global Nominee.

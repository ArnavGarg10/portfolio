I've won 1st Place at the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2025 Mountain View for making shark foraging prediction system using NASA satellite data (read more about it below!), and 2nd Place at the YC x Moss Conversational AI Hackathon for a wearable AI co-pilot built on a Raspberry Pi (see below for more details!). I work across software and hardware, and am especially interested in designing embedded systems that combine Artificial Intelligence with hardware.

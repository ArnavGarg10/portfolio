I'm a freshman at Amador Valley High School with a passion for building things that work in the real world — from custom PCBs for competition submarines to award-winning AI systems. I've won 1st Place at the NASA Space Apps Challenge (Mountain View) for a shark foraging prediction system using NASA satellite data, and 2nd Place at the YC x Moss Conversational AI Hackathon for a wearable AI co-pilot built on a Raspberry Pi. Outside of hackathons, I serve as Electrical Engineer on my school's robotics team (AVBotz), compete in Science Olympiad, coach MathCounts, and lead a Boy Scout troop as Senior Patrol Leader. I work across the full stack — Python, machine learning, hardware design, and web — and I'm driven by turning complex problems into practical, deployable solutions.
